About VSSUT Satsang

VSSUT Satsang

VSSUT Satsang , the spiritual heartbeat of Veer Surendra Sai University of Technology (VSSUT), was founded in the year 2017 by the students of the 2017 batch. Since its inception, the satsang has been a spiritual anchor for students, fostering values, devotion, and community bonding. What began as a humble initiative has now grown into a vibrant community of seekers and followers who walk the path of love, devotion, and truth inspired by Sri Sri Thakur Anukulchandra .

The heart of our satsang is located in Pulaha Hall of Residence, Room No. A-115. This room serves as the prayer room where students gather daily for both morning and evening prayers, creating a serene and divine atmosphere within the campus.

Daily Rituals

Each day, in our sacred prayer room, we continue to offer bhoga to the deity during the morning prayers, symbolizing our gratitude and devotion. The spirit of Satsang at VSSUT is not just about rituals — it is a way of life. It nurtures compassion, community, and a higher purpose among the students.

Weekly Satsang

Every Wednesday, the satsang family gathers for a weekly satsang, where the essence of our spiritual journey is strengthened. These gatherings begin with Guru Bandana, followed by deep discussions on Sri Sri Thakur’s ideology and teachings, inspiring everyone to embody those values in daily life. The sessions are enriched with soulful bhajans, choruses, and kirtans, creating a divine atmosphere. The evening concludes with Prasad Sevan, fostering a sense of unity and shared devotion.

Annual Satsang

A major spiritual highlight each year is the Annual Satsang, hosted in the university’s E-Learning Center. This grand event welcomes students, professors, and dignitaries including the Hon’ble Vice Chancellor. The program features a chief speaker who shares enlightening thoughts on Thakur's life and philosophy, followed by a musical segment that brings calm and joy to the gathering. The satsang concludes with Prasad Sevan for all attendees — a tradition that binds everyone in collective grace.

Albums & Deoghar Trip

Following the Annual Satsang, a special album is created capturing the memories, moments, and bliss of the event. As a continuation of this spiritual journey, a pilgrimage to Deoghar is organized with Satsang alumni — a deeply cherished tradition. Deoghar, located in Jharkhand, holds a special place in our hearts. It is where the holy family of Sri Sri Thakur Anukulchandra resides. During the trip, we offer our heartfelt respects, seek blessings from Acharyadev, and present the Annual Satsang album. In return, we receive valuable guidance for organizing the next year’s satsang with more devotion and discipline.
