About VSSUT Satsang

VSSUT Satsang

Our History

VSSUT Satsang is the spiritual heartbeat of Veer Surendra Sai University of Technology (VSSUT), Burla. It was founded in the year 2017 by students of the 2017 batch with the aim of creating a spiritual atmosphere on the university campus.

What began as a humble initiative has gradually grown into a vibrant family of students, alumni and devotees who continue to walk the path shown by Sree Sree Thakur Anukulchandra.

Prayer Room

The heart of our satsang is located in Pulaha Hall of Residence, Room A-115.

Every morning and evening, students gather in this prayer room for collective prayer, devotional songs and spiritual discussions, creating an atmosphere of peace, unity and devotion throughout the campus.

Daily Rituals

Every day bhoga is offered during the morning prayer as an expression of gratitude and devotion.

Our daily routine is not merely a ritual but a discipline that helps students develop compassion, responsibility, self-control and devotion while balancing academic life.

Weekly Satsang

Every Wednesday the entire satsang family gathers together for the weekly satsang.

The program begins with Guru Bandana, followed by discussions on the teachings and ideals of Sree Sree Thakur Anukulchandra.

Devotional bhajans, chorus singing and kirtans create a joyful spiritual atmosphere before everyone concludes the gathering by accepting Prasad Sevan together.

Annual Satsang

One of the largest spiritual events organised every year is the Annual Satsang conducted at the university's E-Learning Centre.

Students, professors, alumni and distinguished guests, including the Honourable Vice-Chancellor, participate in this celebration.

The event consists of devotional music, inspiring speeches, spiritual discussions and concludes with Prasad Sevan for all participants.

Albums & Deoghar Pilgrimage

Every Annual Satsang is preserved through a commemorative photo album containing memorable moments from the celebration.

After the programme, members of VSSUT Satsang along with alumni undertake a pilgrimage to Deoghar, one of the holiest places associated with Sree Sree Thakur Anukulchandra.

During this pilgrimage, devotees offer their respects, receive blessings and guidance, and present the Annual Satsang album. The guidance received inspires everyone to organise future satsangs with greater dedication, discipline and devotion.
